Most growing companies are quietly bleeding $1,000–$5,000 every month on software they barely use.

It's subscription bloat: a CRM here, an automation tool there, a scheduling app, a billing platform — each one raising prices 10–30% a year, each one built so you can't easily leave. You're not paying for servers. You're paying for lock-in.

Here's the uncomfortable truth we keep proving for clients: for a lot of SMBs, you can replace most of that stack with tools you already pay for — Google Workspace + Apps Script.

## The paradigm shift: own your ecosystem

Apps Script is a serverless JavaScript runtime with direct API access to Gmail, Drive, Calendar, Docs, Forms, and Sheets. That means you can build a CRM, an inventory system, or a billing engine that runs inside your own Google account — at zero marginal server or licensing cost beyond the Workspace seats you already have.

## What you can actually replace

| SaaS | Typical cost | Google Workspace alternative |
| --- | --- | --- |
| CRM (Salesforce, HubSpot) | $150–600/user/mo | Sheets pipeline + Apps Script |
| Inventory (Cin7, Fishbowl) | $100–300/mo | Event-sourced Sheets ledger |
| Billing (FreshBooks, Bill.com) | $50–150/mo | Calendar → Sheet → Google Doc PDFs |
| Scheduling (Calendly) | $8–16/user/mo | Apps Script booking page |
| Automation (Zapier, Make) | $50–500/mo | Native Apps Script triggers |
| BI (Tableau, Looker) | $70–840/mo | Sheets + BigQuery dashboard |

## The highest-ROI patterns

Three replacements pay for themselves fastest:

*   **Headless CRM:** lead form → auto sheet entry → lead scoring → personalized Gmail reply → calendar task → commission attribution.
    
*   **Billing workflow:** calendar time-logging per client → billable-hours math → one-click invoice PDF emailed automatically.
    
*   **WhatsApp sales + inventory:** customer message → real-time Sheets logging → stock check → auto PDF invoice.
    

## The honest part: when NOT to do this

This isn't a silver bullet, and pretending otherwise would cost you. Keep your SaaS if: your team is over ~100 people (Sheets gets laggy, quotas bite), you're in a regulated industry (HIPAA/SOX need certified tools), you depend on deep existing integrations, or you have no in-house Apps Script capacity.

A 30-second gut check — replacing makes sense if 3+ apply: team under ~50, you use under ~30% of the tool's features, it costs over $200/month, your data isn't HIPAA/SOX-bound, and you have Apps Script access.

## The real number

A typical 10-person SMB saves $1,000–$5,000/month, with the build amortizing in 12–18 months. And you own it — no per-seat trap, no annual price hike, your data stays in your Drive.
